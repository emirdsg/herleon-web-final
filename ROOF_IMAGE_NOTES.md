# Referencias visuales de techos

Método: ImageGen integrado. Ilustraciones de referencia, no documentación constructiva ni proyectos atribuidos a Herleon.

Prompt común: Use case: product-mockup. One photorealistic landscape 3:2 architectural reference image for Herleon glass and aluminum installation website. Natural daylight, warm off-white and sand architecture with green foliage. Straight realistic construction and subtle shadow patterns, camera focuses on overhead roof geometry while showing surrounding terrace context. No people, no text, no labels, no logos, no collage. Illustrative reference, not engineering documentation.

## damero

Archivo: public/assets/techos-damero-referencia.png

Escena: A fixed dark bronze aluminum sol y sombra pergola roof in an unmistakable checkerboard damero pattern: square modules filled with parallel narrow slats, alternating their slat direction by 90 degrees from square to square, woven chessboard appearance visible overhead, some sky between slats. Contemporary cream house terrace, patio seating below. The checkerboard roof is the main subject.

## corredizo

Escena: A sliding retractable charcoal aluminum sol y sombra pergola roof above a modern neutral patio.
